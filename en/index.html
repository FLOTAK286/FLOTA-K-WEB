    FAQ

    Before you book

    How do I book?

    Message us on WhatsApp at +56 9 2227 5399 or email gerencia@fk-servicios.com with your trip details (address, date, time, flight number, passengers and luggage). We confirm through the same channel and, before the trip, send you your driver’s details and a link to follow the transfer live.

    How far in advance should I book?

    The sooner the better, to secure your time. For early-morning flights, book at least the night before. For a same-day transfer, message us and we will check availability.

    What if my flight is delayed?

    We track your flight and wait for you from landing: 45 minutes for domestic flights and 1 hour for international flights, at no extra cost. If the wait goes beyond that, only the additional airport parking fee is added.

    Do you have child seats?

    We do not provide child seats. As a taxi service, they are not required by Chilean regulations.

    Can I travel with my pet?

    Yes, pets are welcome travelling in their carrier. We cannot transport pets without a carrier.

    How do I pay?

    By bank transfer or card. Companies can pay with a monthly invoice under an agreement.

    Do you go outside Santiago?

    Yes, at a fixed price to destinations such as Valparaíso, Viña del Mar, Rancagua, Los Andes, San Felipe, Melipilla and Farellones. Look them up in the fare finder above. Between eastern Santiago (Las Condes, Vitacura, Lo Barnechea, Providencia, La Reina, Ñuñoa) and Viña del Mar or Valparaíso: CLP $170,000 by car and CLP $240,000 by van, each way. Book on WhatsApp.
